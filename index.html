Walk inside it before it’s built.

I’m Waseem, a 3D modeler and animator. I turn drawings and ideas into environments, interiors and product renders that clients can walk through, judge and approve.

Every project starts as a flat drawing and ends as a place you can stand in.

This portfolio follows that journey. You’ll step into a full 360° service station, walk the floors of an office tower, hold the products up to the light, and finish at the very first donut I rendered. Everything below is clickable, and every 360° view is live.

Chapter one

Worlds

Golden Petroleum needed a full service station, from forecourt to warehouse, that stakeholders could judge before a single wall went up. So instead of five camera angles, I built a place you can look around in. Drag it.

Golden Petroleum
Loading 360°…

The challenge

A busy, multi-bay service station with a strong brand: how do you show scale, flow and atmosphere in one go?

The approach

Brand red and signal yellow drive the floor markings, pillars and signage. Each zone, from lounge to bays to warehouse, was rendered as a full panorama.

The result

A client can stand at the entrance, turn to the service pits, then jump to the warehouse, all in the browser, on any device.

Now walk it yourself

The same station, rebuilt as a real-time 3D scene. Move through the forecourt, up to the counter and between the service pits, with nothing pre-rendered.

Step inside the station

Chapter two

Spaces

Interiors are about how a room feels at 4 pm on a Tuesday. Light, material and the way furniture meets a wall. These are three buildings, designed floor by floor.

Quaid Venture Tower

A full tower, from a basement studio to a ground-floor co-working hall and the mezzanine offices above. Pick a floor and walk through it.

Tracking

A branch for a Saudi-made tracking-device brand. Display plinths for the hardware, a service desk and teal wall graphics that explain the product line without a salesperson.

GSG

Workspace, reception and an exhibition corner for Global Solutions Group, including a display wall for Epic Semi’s AI-chip hardware.

Objects
